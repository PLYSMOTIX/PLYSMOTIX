Privacy Policy

Video Player  •  Effective date: 14 Aug 2026

This policy explains what information Video Player ("the app", "we", "our") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. Video Player is developed by Plysmotix Tech Lab.

1. Summary

Video Player is built to work without an account or sign-in. It plays video files already on your device and video URLs you add yourself. We do not operate a server that stores your videos, your playlists, or your viewing history — that information stays on your device. The app does display ads and checks for app updates through Google Play, both of which are described below.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No account creation, sign-in, name, email address, or phone number is required or requested by the app itself.
  • We do not operate a backend server, so we do not receive or store your video files, playlists, favorites, or watch history — these are saved only in the app's local storage on your device.
  • We do not sell personal information.

3. Information handled on your device

The app stores the following locally, using standard Android app storage. It is not transmitted to us:

  • Your favorites, playlists, "recently played" list, and per-video resume position
  • Video URLs you've added
  • App preferences (theme, sort order, grid/list view)
  • Downloaded video files you've explicitly chosen to save for offline playback, stored in the app's private storage area

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

4. Permissions the app requests, and why

PermissionPurpose
Videos / media (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)To list and play video files already stored on your device. Used only to display your own library inside the app.
Internet / network stateTo play video URLs you add, load ads, check for app updates, and cast to a nearby device.
NotificationsTo show download progress when you choose to save a video for offline playback.
Foreground serviceRequired by Android so an in-progress download keeps running reliably while you use other apps.

5. Advertising

The app shows a banner ad on the video-browsing screen only — never during video playback — served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, and may use this data consistent with Google's own privacy practices. We do not receive your personal browsing data from AdMob; we only receive aggregate reporting about ad performance.

Where required by law (for example, users in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows a consent form before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your ad consent choice at any time from the app's menu ("Ad privacy options").

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Google Play services used by this app

  • In-app updates — the app checks with Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if so, offers to install it. This uses Google Play's own update-check API and does not involve a separate server of ours.
  • Google Cast — if you choose to cast a video to a TV or other Cast-enabled device, playback is handed off to that device using Google's Cast framework. Only video URLs you added yourself can be cast; local device files are never sent anywhere.

7. Children's privacy

Video Player is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will address it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Since app data (favorites, playlists, downloads, preferences) lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of this data on any server.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@plysmotixtechlab.com.
