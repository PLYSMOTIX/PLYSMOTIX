Privacy Policy for Wellbeing

Last updated: 17 Aug 2026

Wellbeing is built to be private by default. Your mood check-ins, journal entries, and photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any server we control. This policy explains what limited data is handled by the third-party services the app relies on for ads and app updates.

1. Who we are

Wellbeing is developed by Plysmotix Tech Lab (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at support@plysmotixtechlab.com with any questions about this policy or your data.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is created and stored locally in the app's private database and files. It is never transmitted to us:

  • Mood, energy, and anxiety check-ins, including any notes and tags you add
  • Journal entries, including any photo you attach
  • Custom tags and custom breathing patterns you create
  • App settings: reminder times, theme choice, app-lock preference

This data is only removed if you delete it yourself, use the app's “Delete all data” option, or uninstall the app. Automatic and manual backups you create are saved as files on your own device (or wherever you choose to save them) and are likewise not sent to us.

3. App lock

If you enable the app-lock setting, Wellbeing asks your device to verify your existing screen lock (PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint, or face) using Android's standard authentication system. Wellbeing never sees, collects, or stores your PIN, password, or any biometric data itself — that is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

4. Third-party services

Wellbeing uses a small number of Google services to keep the app free and to deliver updates. These services may process limited technical data as described below.

4.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

Wellbeing shows a small banner ad, served through Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads SDK). To do this, AdMob may collect and process technical identifiers such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and general ad-interaction data, in order to select and measure ads. In regions where required by law (the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), Wellbeing asks for your consent — via Google's User Messaging Platform — before any ad is requested, and only requests non-personalized or personalized ads according to the choice you make. You can review or change this choice at any time from Settings → Ads & privacy → Manage ad privacy choices.

4.2 App updates — Google Play Core

If you installed Wellbeing from Google Play, the app may check with Google Play's in-app update service to see whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by Google Play and does not involve any of your check-in or journal data.

4.3 Google Play Services

The above services run on top of Google Play Services, which is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy.

6. Data you choose to export

Wellbeing lets you export your data as a JSON backup file or a PDF report, entirely at your own initiative. These files are saved wherever you choose (your device storage, or a location you pick via the system file picker) and are your responsibility from that point on — MindEase does not upload them anywhere.

7. Children's privacy

Wellbeing is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete any check-in or journal entry individually, or use “Delete all data” in Settings to erase everything at once.
  • You can turn off ads-related consent or change your ad-privacy choice at any time (Settings → Ads & privacy).
  • You can turn off reminders, app lock, or auto-backup at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local database and files from your device.
  • Depending on your region, you may have rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA regarding data processed by our third-party ad partners — see Google's Privacy Policy linked above, and their ads & data page for how to exercise those rights with them directly.

9. Data security

Sensitive local settings (such as your app-lock preference) are stored using Android's encrypted storage. Because your check-in and journal data never leaves your device through Wellbeing itself, its security depends significantly on your own device's security (screen lock, OS updates, etc.).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature that changes what data is processed. We'll update the “Last updated” date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@plysmotixtechlab.com.
