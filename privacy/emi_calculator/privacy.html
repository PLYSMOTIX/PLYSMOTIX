Privacy Policy

EMI Calculator

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This policy explains what information EMI Calculator ("the app," "we," "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app's core purpose — calculating loan EMIs, interest, and amortization schedules — runs entirely on your device. The data practices below relate to advertising and app-update functionality only.

1. Information we do not collect

Your loan amounts, interest rates, tenure inputs, and calculated results are processed only on your device and are never transmitted to us or stored on any server. We have no account system and do not require you to sign in.

2. Advertising and consent

The app is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Where required by applicable law (for example, the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or similar regional rules), the app shows a consent form — built on Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) — before loading any personalized ads. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time from the "Privacy options" link in the app.

Depending on your consent choice, Google and its advertising partners may process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID / device identifiersAd delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Coarse device & app informationAd formatting and basic analytics
Approximate location (region-level)Determining which consent rules apply, and ad relevance where permitted

We do not control how Google or its partners use this data beyond what's described in Google's Partner Sites policy and the AdMob data disclosure. We encourage you to review those directly.

3. App updates

The app uses Google Play's In-App Update service to check whether a newer version has been published and, if so, to offer an in-app update. This check is handled by the Google Play Store app already installed on your device and does not involve any additional data collection by us.

4. Children's privacy

EMI Calculator is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If personalized ads are shown, we mark ad requests as not intended for users known to be under the age of consent in their region, consistent with our AdMob configuration.

5. Data retention and security

Since calculation inputs and results never leave your device, there is nothing for us to retain or secure on our end. Advertising identifiers are retained according to Google's own policies, linked above, not ours.

6. Your choices

  • Open "Privacy options" inside the app at any time to change your ad consent choice.
  • Reset your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads entirely, from your device's OS-level settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored calculator data immediately.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
