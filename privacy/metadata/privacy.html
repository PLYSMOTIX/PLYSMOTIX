Privacy Policy

EXIF Studio  •  Effective date: 17 Aug 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information EXIF Studio ("the app", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and the choices available to you. EXIF Studio is designed so that your photos and their metadata stay on your device — we do not operate a server that receives or stores your images.

1. Information We Access

1.1 Photos you choose to open

When you select a photo to view, edit, crop, compress, or watermark, the app reads that photo directly from your device's storage (or the system photo picker) and processes it locally, on your device. Edited or exported copies are saved back to your device's gallery or shared using the share options you choose. EXIF Studio does not upload your photos to any server operated by us, and we never see or retain copies of your images.

1.2 Photo metadata (EXIF data)

The app reads and, if you choose to edit it, writes standard EXIF metadata embedded in your photos — for example camera make/model, date taken, exposure settings, and GPS coordinates already present in the file. This happens entirely on your device.

1.3 Location data

EXIF Studio only accesses your device's location when you explicitly tap "Tag Current Location" inside the EXIF editor. When you do, the app requests your device's current coordinates from the operating system's location services and writes them directly into the selected photo's EXIF data. This location data is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the app — it is stored only inside the photo file you are editing, under your control.

1.4 Advertising data

EXIF Studio shows banner and occasional full-screen ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers (such as your advertising ID), approximate location, and other data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy linked below. We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for personalized advertising where required (for example, under GDPR in the EEA/UK), and you can review or change that choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.

1.5 App updates

EXIF Studio uses the Google Play Core library to check for and install app updates directly through the Google Play Store. This relies on your existing Google Play account and Play Store app on your device — EXIF Studio does not operate any separate update servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require account creation, sign-in, or any personal profile.
  • We do not operate servers that store your photos, EXIF data, or location history.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, call history, or other apps' data.

5. Data Retention

Working copies of photos you edit are stored temporarily in the app's private cache storage on your device while you use each tool, and are cleared automatically by the operating system or when you clear the app's cache. Photos you explicitly save are placed in your device's gallery, where they remain under your control like any other photo.

6. Children's Privacy

EXIF Studio is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

7. Your Choices

  • Ad consent: Review or change your advertising consent choice at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Privacy Options").
  • Ad frequency: Control how often full-screen ads appear, including turning them off, from Settings.
  • Location: The location permission is only requested when you actively choose to tag a photo; you can deny it and keep using every other feature of the app.
  • Device permissions: You can review or revoke any permission at any time from your device's system Settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the app's features or in applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
