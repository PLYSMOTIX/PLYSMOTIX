Privacy Policy

Nearby File Share  •  Effective date: July 23 2026

Short version

Nearby File Share lets you send and receive files directly with people near you using Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi — your files never pass through our servers, because we don't have any. The app does not require an account and does not collect the content of the files you share. It requests Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi, and (on older Android versions) location permissions only because Android requires them to scan for nearby devices — we do not use location data for any other purpose and do not track your location. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers as described below, and you're asked for consent to this where required by law (e.g. in the EEA/UK).

1. Who we are

Nearby File Share ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Plysmotix Tech Lab. You can reach us at support@plysmotixtechlab.com with any questions about this policy or your data.

2. What the App does

The App uses Google's Nearby Connections API to discover other nearby devices running the App (over Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi‑Fi Direct/Hotspot) and to transfer files you choose directly between devices. There is no cloud server, no account system, and no upload of your files to us or to Google as part of this transfer.

3. Information we collect

CategoryWhat, and why
Files you choose to send Transferred directly, device‑to‑device, over Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi. Never sent to our servers — we don't operate any.
Device / nearby scanning data To find nearby devices, Android's Nearby Connections API uses Bluetooth signals and (on Android 12 and below) requires the Location permission to be granted, purely because the OS ties Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi scanning APIs to that permission. We do not read, store, or transmit your GPS location, and we do not use this permission for any location-based purpose.
Share history Filenames, file sizes, peer device names, and timestamps of your transfers are stored locally on your device only (not uploaded anywhere) so you can review your own history. You can clear it at any time from the History tab.
Advertising data The App shows banner ads via Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, device information, and general (non-precise) location to serve and measure ads, subject to the consent choices described in Section 5.
Crash / diagnostic data Standard Google Play services (Play Core in-app updates) may report basic diagnostic and update-status data to Google to power the app-update feature.

4. Permissions explained

  • Bluetooth (scan / connect / advertise): required to discover and connect to nearby devices.
  • Wi‑Fi state / Nearby Wi‑Fi devices: required for faster, higher-bandwidth file transfer over Wi‑Fi Direct.
  • Location (Android 12 and earlier only): required by Android itself to perform Bluetooth/Wi‑Fi scans on these OS versions. Not used by us to determine or store your whereabouts.
  • Storage / Media access: used only to let you pick a file to send and to save a file someone sends you into your device's Downloads folder.

5. Advertising & consent

We use Google AdMob to show banner ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect your consent for personalized/non-personalized ads where required — for example under the EU/EEA and UK GDPR frameworks. You'll see a consent form the first time this applies to you, and you can change your choice later from the app's privacy settings. See Google's own policy for how it uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

6. Third-party services we rely on

  • Google Play Services — Nearby Connections API (device discovery & transfer)
  • Google Play Core — in-app update checks
  • Google AdMob & Google User Messaging Platform — ads and consent

Each of these is operated by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data retention

Share history is stored locally on your device until you clear it yourself (History tab → Clear) or uninstall the App. We do not retain any copy of it elsewhere, because we don't operate servers that could.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export data associated with you (for example, ad identifiers held by Google). Because the App itself stores your share history only on-device, you can exercise most of these rights simply by clearing history or uninstalling the App. For advertising data held by Google, see Google's Ad Settings.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
