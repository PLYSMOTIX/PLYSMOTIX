The short version

Plysmotix Connect has no server, no account system, and no internet connection for any core feature. Here's what that means in practice:

  • Your messages, contacts, groups, and identity key are stored only on your device — we never see them, because there's nowhere for them to go.
  • We don't run analytics, don't show ads, and don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any to begin with.
  • The permissions the app requests (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, camera, microphone) are used solely to make on-device features work — never for tracking.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it ever stored.

01Overview

Plysmotix Connect ("the app," "we," "us") is an offline mesh messaging app for Android. It connects directly to nearby devices over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi to send encrypted messages, with no internet connection, server, or user account involved in any core feature. This policy explains what little data the app handles, where it lives, and why each permission exists.

02Data we don't collect

Because the app has no server or account system, there is no mechanism for us to collect any of the following, and we don't:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials — the app never asks for them
  • Your messages, photos, voice notes, or files — these are encrypted and travel device-to-device only
  • Your contacts, groups, or channels — stored only in the app's local database on your phone
  • Your location — the app does not access GPS or network-based location, on any Android version
  • Analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or advertising identifiers

There is simply no Plysmotix Connect server for any of this to be sent to.

The app stores the following locally, in its own private app storage, encrypted at rest using Android's Keystore-backed encryption where the data is sensitive:

05Third-party services

The app uses two Google-provided libraries to work, both of which run on your device:

  • Google Nearby Connections API — powers the Bluetooth/Wi-Fi device discovery and connection. It operates peer-to-peer and does not require or use an internet connection to function.
  • Google ML Kit / Play Services code scanner — provides the QR code scanning UI when adding a contact.

Both are part of Google Play Services, which operates under Google's own Privacy Policy — outside our control, since Play Services is present on the device independent of this app. We don't configure either library to send analytics or usage data, and neither is used for anything beyond the specific on-device feature it provides.

The app itself makes no network requests of its own and connects to no other third-party service.

06Data sharing

We don't share, sell, rent, or trade any data, because we don't have any to share — nothing you do in the app is ever transmitted to us. Messages, media, and profile information you choose to send go directly and only to the specific device(s) you're communicating with, encrypted end-to-end.

07Retention and deletion

All data described above stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you delete it yourself using the app's own controls (for example, leaving a group or channel, or blocking a contact). Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your identity key, contacts, messages, and all other locally stored data — there is no copy anywhere else to delete, because none was ever made.

08Security

Direct messages use a forward-secret Double Ratchet (ECDH key exchange plus AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption), meaning encryption keys rotate as you converse and a compromised device can't decrypt past messages. Your identity key is stored using Android's Keystore-backed encrypted storage. Group and channel messages use a shared AES-256 key distributed only to participants.

No software is perfectly secure, and this app has not undergone an independent third-party security audit. We describe its cryptographic design honestly rather than making unqualified security claims.

09Children's privacy

Plysmotix Connect is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children — consistent with the rest of this policy, we don't collect personal information from anyone, of any age, since there is no server to collect it on.

10Your choices

  • Revoke permissions anytime in Android Settings → Apps → Plysmotix Connect → Permissions. Revoking a permission simply disables the feature that needs it (for example, revoking Camera disables QR scanning).
  • Block a contact in-app to stop receiving messages, media, or profile updates from them.
  • Leave a group or channel at any time from its options menu.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app.
