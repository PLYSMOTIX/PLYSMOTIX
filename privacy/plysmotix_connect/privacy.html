Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Mesh Communication App ("the App") handles your information while using Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct, and local Wi-Fi mesh networking features.

Information We Collect

The App is designed to operate primarily over local peer-to-peer mesh connections and does not require user registration.

The App may access:

  • Bluetooth permissions for discovering and connecting nearby devices.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices for peer-to-peer communication.
  • Network information required to establish mesh communication.
  • Device name (only to identify nearby devices).

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or messages for our servers.

How Messages Work

Messages are exchanged directly between nearby devices through Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct, or local mesh networking.

If the App provides optional relay functionality through nearby devices, messages are forwarded only to deliver them to recipients and are not stored permanently by us.

Internet Connection

The App is designed to work offline whenever possible. Some optional features or future updates may require an internet connection.

Data Storage

Messages and application settings are stored locally on your device. The App does not upload your conversations to our servers unless a future feature explicitly requires cloud synchronization and you choose to enable it.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Bluetooth
  • Bluetooth Scan
  • Bluetooth Connect
  • Nearby Wi-Fi Devices
  • Network Access
  • Notification Permission (optional)
  • Foreground Service (if required for continuous mesh communication)

These permissions are used solely for enabling mesh networking and message delivery.

Encryption

Where supported, messages transmitted between devices may be encrypted to improve communication security.

Third-Party Services

The App does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. If optional services such as crash reporting or analytics are enabled in future versions, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to help protect locally stored data and communications. However, no electronic communication method can guarantee complete security.

Your Rights

Since your data is stored locally, you can delete your conversations and application data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.
