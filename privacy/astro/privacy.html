In short: Plysmotix Astro calculates your kundli, dasha, panchang, and other astrological details entirely on your device. Your birth details are stored locally on your phone and are not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by us.

1. Information You Provide

When you create a birth profile, you provide:

  • Name
  • Date and time of birth
  • Place of birth (name, latitude, longitude)

This information is stored only in a local database on your device (using Android's Room/SQLite storage) and is used solely to calculate your astrological charts and predictions. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Plysmotix Astro does not collect, and has no access to:

  • Your birth profile data (name, birth date/time/place)
  • Your device's precise location (you enter birth coordinates manually)
  • Contacts, photos, messages, or other personal files

3. Sharing a Chart

If you use the "Share Chart as Image" feature, an image of your kundli chart is generated on your device and handed to the Android share sheet. It is only sent to whichever app or contact you personally choose at that point — we have no visibility into, or involvement in, that transfer.
