Privacy Policy

Cycle Care: Period Tracker — Womens Health

Effective date: 23 July 2026  •  Last updated: 23 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plysmotix ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Cycle Care: Period Tracker mobile application (the "App"). We built this app around one core principle: your cycle data belongs on your device, not on our servers.

In short: all health data you enter (period dates, symptoms, notes, etc.) is stored only in a local database on your phone. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to this data. Ads are on by default to support the free App, always behind a consent check first, and you can turn them off anytime in Settings — every other feature works without the internet either way.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not operate a backend for the App. As a result, we do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share:

  • Period dates, cycle length, flow intensity, symptoms, mood, or notes you log
  • Your name, email address, or account information (the App has no account/login)
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Any health information you enter into the App

This data never leaves your device through any feature we built, except if you explicitly choose to export/share it yourself (e.g. via your device's share sheet).

2. Data stored locally on your device

The App uses a local database (Room/SQLite) stored in the App's private storage area on your device. This includes:

DataPurpose
Period start/end dates, flow intensity, symptoms, mood, notes Powering the calendar, cycle predictions, and history screens
Cycle length & period length averages Predicting your next period and fertile window
App settings (reminder toggles, app lock toggle, ads toggle) Remembering your preferences

This local data is removed if you uninstall the App, or immediately if you use the "Delete all my data" option in Settings.

3. Ads & the consent process

Ads are on by default to help keep the App free, and can be turned off anytime in Settings. Ads are the only feature that uses your internet connection — every other feature works fully offline.

Before any ad is ever requested — whether that's on first launch or any time the "Show ads" setting is on — we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine what consent is legally required in your region (e.g. under GDPR in the EEA/UK) and, where required, show you a consent form first. An ad is never displayed until that consent step has actually completed and allows it. You can turn ads off at any time in Settings, which immediately stops further ad requests.

6. Data security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends significantly on your device's own security (screen lock, OS updates, etc.). The App offers an optional app-lock setting for an extra layer of protection. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Access & export: all your data is visible within the App itself (History/Stats screens).
  • Deletion: use "Delete all my data" in Settings, or simply uninstall the App.
  • Ads opt-out: turn off "Show ads" in Settings at any time.
  • Regional rights (GDPR/CCPA and similar laws): because we do not collect or store your data on any server, most data-subject requests (access, deletion, portability) are already satisfied by the controls in the App itself. For any other privacy request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention & deletion

Local data persists on your device until you delete it in-app or uninstall the App. We hold no copy of it anywhere else, so there is nothing further for us to retain or delete on our end.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the App's store listing.
