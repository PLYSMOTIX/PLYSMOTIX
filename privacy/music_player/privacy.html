Privacy Policy

App: Music Player  •  Effective date: 2 Aug 2026

Plysmotix Tech Lab ("we," "us," or "our") developed Music Player (the "App") as a free, ad-supported music player. This policy explains what information the App accesses, what we do (and don't do) with it, and your choices.

1. The short version

The App plays music files that are already stored on your device. Your music library is read locally and is never uploaded to us or to any server we control. We don't require an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers ourselves. The App does show banner ads through Google AdMob, which independently collects some device and advertising data as described in Section 4. Where required (for example, users in the EEA/UK), the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's own consent tool — see Section 4 for details and how to change your choice later.

2. Information the App accesses on your device

  • Audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / storage permission): used only to list and play the songs already on your device. This data stays on your device and is read directly from Android's MediaStore each time the App scans your library.
  • Playlists, favorites, and play history: stored in a local database on your device only. We do not have access to this data, and it is not transmitted anywhere by the App itself.
  • Home screen widget: if you add the App's widget to your home screen, it displays the title/artist of whatever is currently playing and lets you control playback. This is the same local information described above, shown in a different place — the widget does not collect or transmit anything on its own.
  • Network access: used for two things: (a) checking Google Play for app updates, and (b) loading and displaying banner ads. Network access is not used to upload your music or your listening activity.

3. Permissions the App requests, and why

  • Read media / storage — to find and play audio files on your device.
  • Internet / network state — for ads and update checks.
  • Foreground service / media playback — so music keeps playing when the app is in the background or the screen is off.
  • Notifications — to show the playback controls notification while music plays.
  • Wake lock — to prevent playback from being interrupted while the device is briefly waking or dozing.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Music Player → Permissions.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob. The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, general location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. We do not control what AdMob collects or how long it retains that data. You can learn more, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads Settings (opt out of personalized ads)

Ads consent (Google User Messaging Platform). Before showing any ad, the App asks Google whether your account/region requires a consent prompt (this generally applies to users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland). If it does, the App shows a consent form — built and served by Google, not by us — before any ad is requested, and no ad loads until that's resolved. If you're shown this prompt, you can change your answer at any time from the "Privacy options" control in the App's library screen (it only appears when applicable to you).

Google Play services (in-app updates). The App uses Google Play's in-app update feature to check whether a newer version is available. This check is handled by Google Play services under Google's own privacy policy linked above; it does not send us any personal data.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal files. We do not require account creation or sign-in. We do not sell personal data, because we do not collect any to sell.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will address it.

7. Data security

Your music library, playlists, favorites, and play history are stored locally on your device, using the same protections Android applies to any app's private data. Because we don't operate a server that stores this information, there's no remote database of your listening habits for us to secure — or to lose.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature that changes what data the App accesses. We'll update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how the App handles data? Reach out at support@plysmotixtechlab.com.
