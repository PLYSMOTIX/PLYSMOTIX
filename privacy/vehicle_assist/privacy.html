Privacy Policy

Fuel Manager · Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Fuel Manager ("the App", "we", "our") is a vehicle trip and fuel-tracking app. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it's used, and — most importantly — what we don't do with it. The short version: your trip and fuel data stays on your device. We don't require an account, we don't run our own servers to collect your data, and we don't sell anything.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS to track trips you actively start, recording:

  • Latitude/longitude points along your route
  • Speed at each point
  • Timestamp of each point

Location is only collected while a trip is running. If you grant "Allow all the time" (background) location access, tracking continues if you lock your phone or switch apps mid-trip; if you choose "Only while using the app," tracking pauses when the App isn't in the foreground. You can use the App entirely via manual trip entry instead, in which case GPS is never used.

1.2 Vehicle & Fuel Data You Enter

  • Vehicle name, type, and fuel type
  • Odometer readings
  • Fuel quantity, cost, and fill-up date
  • Manually entered trip distance/duration, if you use that mode

1.3 Device Permissions

PermissionWhy the App requests it
Location (fine / coarse) To track trip distance, speed, and route while a trip is active.
Background location Optional. Keeps trip tracking running if you lock your screen or switch apps mid-trip.
Notifications To show live trip progress while tracking, and optional fuel-log reminders.
Foreground service Required by Android to keep GPS tracking running reliably and transparently (shown via a persistent notification) while a trip is active.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All trip, vehicle, and fuel data is stored locally on your device in a private app database. We do not operate cloud servers that store your personal trip or fuel history, and the App does not require creating an account. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted along with it unless you've exported a copy.

When you use the Export feature, a CSV file is generated and handed to Android's standard share sheet so you can send it via email, save it to cloud storage, or share it elsewhere — that transfer is controlled entirely by you and the destination app you choose, not by us.

3. Third-Party Services

The route map feature uses the Google Maps SDK to display your trip route. When the map is shown, Google may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This only occurs when you open a trip's route map — it is not used elsewhere in the App.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or data brokers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data leaves your device only when:

  • You explicitly use the Export feature and choose where to send the file, or
  • The Google Maps SDK renders a route map, per Google's own privacy policy (section 3).

5. Your Choices

  • Location access can be granted, downgraded to "while using the app," or revoked entirely in your device Settings at any time. Manual trip entry works without any location permission.
  • Notifications (trip tracking and fuel reminders) can be disabled in your device Settings or, for fuel reminders specifically, from within the App's Settings screen.
  • Your data can be exported at any time, and permanently deleted by uninstalling the App or deleting individual trips/entries in-app.

6. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally rather than transmitted to us, its security is primarily governed by your device's own protections (screen lock, disk encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's OS up to date.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App's features change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
