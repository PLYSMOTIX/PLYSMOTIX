🛡️ Safety App — Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone. That's the whole design.

Effective date: 1 Jan 2026] · Last updated: 14 July 2026

The short version

Safety App has no backend server. Nothing you enter is uploaded to us, because there's no "us" to upload it to.
Your emergency contacts, location, medical ID, and SOS history are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
When you trigger SOS, your phone sends an SMS and/or shares a link directly to the contacts you chose — the app itself never sees or stores that message on any server.
We don't run analytics, ad SDKs, or trackers in this app.
Audio evidence clips are recorded and saved locally; they're only shared if you explicitly tap "Share."
You can delete your data at any time — clear it in-app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

1. Introduction

Safety App ("the app", "we") is a personal emergency-response application covering women's safety, medical emergencies, accidents, and general panic situations. This policy explains what information the app accesses, why it needs that access, and — most importantly — where that information actually goes. It applies to the Android application distributed on Google Play under the package name in.plysmotix.womensafety.

The single most important fact in this document: the app does not operate a server, database, or cloud backend of any kind. Every feature described below — SOS messaging, location, contacts, medical ID, audio evidence, check-in timers — runs and stores its data entirely on your device, using your phone's own SMS, dialer, and storage capabilities. We built it this way because a safety app is the last place you want your most sensitive information sitting on someone else's server.

2. Data the app handles

All of the following is created by you, stored locally on your device, and never transmitted to us:

Data typeWhat it isWhere it lives
Emergency contacts Phone numbers you choose to add (optionally picked from your device's Contacts app) Local app storage only
Location GPS/network coordinates, fetched when you open the app and when SOS is triggered Local app storage; sent only inside the SOS text message to contacts you chose
SOS message content Your custom message text and selected emergency category Local app storage; sent via your device's own SMS function
Audio evidence clips Short audio recordings captured automatically during an SOS, if you've enabled this feature Local app-private storage; only leaves your device if you tap Share
Medical ID Blood type, allergies, medications, conditions, emergency doctor contact (all optional fields you fill in) Local app storage; included in SOS texts only if you enable that option
SOS history log Timestamps and delivery status (sent/failed/pending) of past SOS events Local on-device database
App settings Feature toggles, repeat interval, dark mode preference, default emergency category Local app storage
We don't ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use this app — so we don't collect any of that either.

4. How your data is used

  • To send your SOS. Your location and message are combined into a text sent via your device's SMS to the contacts you selected.
  • To let you review past activity. SOS History and Audio Evidence screens read directly from local on-device storage.
  • To personalize emergency messages. Your chosen SOS category and optional Medical ID are used to build a more useful message for responders.
  • To run optional automation you turn on — repeat SOS, check-in timers, shake/fall detection — all evaluated locally on your device.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the safety features you enable.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, because we don't have a copy of it to share — it never leaves your device except through actions you directly initiate:

  • SMS sent to contacts you chose, via your device's own carrier/SMS app
  • A Google Maps link included in that SMS, generated locally from your coordinates
  • Audio clips or app links you explicitly choose to share via Android's share sheet
  • The "Find Nearby Help" feature opens your device's default Maps app with a search query — no data about you is sent to us in the process

6. Storage, security, and retention

All app data is stored in Android's app-private internal storage, which is sandboxed by the operating system and inaccessible to other apps without root access. Sensitive screens (like your saved contacts) can optionally be protected behind your device's biometric or PIN lock via Android's BiometricPrompt — we never receive or store your fingerprint, face data, or PIN; that's handled entirely by your device's OS.

Data is retained for as long as the app is installed, or until you manually delete it (e.g., deleting a log entry or audio clip). Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data permanently, per standard Android behavior.

7. Your choices and rights

Because everything lives on your device, you're already in full control:

  • Access: open any in-app screen (Contacts, SOS History, Audio Evidence, Medical ID) to see exactly what's stored.
  • Correction: edit your contacts, medical ID, or settings at any time.
  • Deletion: delete individual SOS logs or audio clips from within the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything at once.
  • Permission control: revoke any permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Safety App → Permissions. Note that revoking SMS or Location will prevent core SOS functionality from working.
Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, requests like "please delete my data from your servers" aren't applicable — there's nothing on our end to delete. If you believe this is inaccurate for a specific build of the app, please contact us using the details in Section 11.

8. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from children. As all data is stored locally and there is no account system, we have no mechanism to knowingly identify or collect data from any user by age.

9. Third-party services we rely on

The app integrates a small number of Google/Android platform services to function. These operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play In-App Updates — checks for new app versions via Google Play services.
  • Google Play Services Location (Fused Location Provider) — used to obtain your device's location for SOS.
  • Android biometric/keystore APIs — used for the optional Contacts screen lock, handled entirely on-device.

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services that transmit data off-device.

If you later add Firebase, Crashlytics, an ads SDK, or any analytics tool, this section and the summary above must be updated to reflect that — this policy is only accurate for the on-device-only version of the app described here.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds cloud backup, an account system, or analytics — we will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify users via an in-app notice before the change takes effect.
