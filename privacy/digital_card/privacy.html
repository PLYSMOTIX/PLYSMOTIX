Privacy Policy

Digital Card — Digital Visiting Card App

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Card ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. Digital Card is designed to keep your data on your own device wherever possible — this policy tells you exactly what leaves the device and when.

Effective date: 2 Aug 2026

Developer / Publisher: Plysmotix Tech Lab

1. Summary

Digital Card stores the business cards you create locally on your device. We do not operate a server, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your card data. The only data that leaves your device is the information the App's advertising partner (Google AdMob) collects to show ads, described in Section 4.

2. Information You Provide

When you create a card in Digital Card, you may enter:

  • Full name, job title, and company
  • Phone number, email address, and physical address
  • Website and social media links (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram)
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one

For cards you receive from other people (via QR scan, NFC, or importing from your phone's Contacts), you may also add a private note to help you remember the context — e.g. where you met. This note is visible only to you and is stored the same way as everything else described below.

This information is stored only in a local database on your device. It is not uploaded to any server we operate. It leaves your device only when you choose to share a card — for example, by generating a QR code, tapping NFC, sending a share link, exporting a vCard file, or saving a card to your phone's Contacts app. In each of these cases, the data goes directly to the person or app you shared it with, not through us.

3. Permissions the App Requests

PermissionWhy Digital Card asks for it
Camera To scan a QR code when importing someone else's card. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not stored or transmitted.
NFC To write your card to a physical NFC tag or exchange it with another phone via tap. Data is only sent when you actively start a tap-to-share action.
Contacts (read/write) To let you import an existing phone contact as a new card, or export a Digital Card card into your phone's Contacts app. Only triggered by your explicit action; contact data is never sent anywhere else.
Photos / Media To let you pick a profile photo for a card. The selected image is copied into the app's private storage on your device.
Storage (Android 9 and below only) To save a card's QR code to your photo gallery, if you choose to. On Android 10 and above this needs no permission at all, thanks to scoped storage.
Biometric hardware (fingerprint/face) Only used if you turn on App Lock and choose fingerprint/face unlock. Digital Card never sees or stores your biometric data — the match happens entirely inside Android's own secure system prompt, and the App only receives a yes/no result.
Install unknown apps Only requested if you choose to install an update package the App finds on your device (see Section 6). Not used for anything else.
Internet / Network state Used solely to load banner advertisements (Section 4). Digital Card's own features — creating, storing, and sharing cards — work fully offline.

4. Advertising

Digital Card displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location, and other technical data to show and measure ads, and may use this data for ad personalization depending on your device and regional settings.

This data collection is performed by Google, not by Digital Card directly. You can review how Google handles this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at:

  • policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • adssettings.google.com

5. Backup Files

If you use the "Backup Cards" feature, Digital Card writes a JSON file containing your card data to a location you choose on your device (via Android's system file picker). This file is created and stored entirely under your control — we have no access to it, and it is not uploaded anywhere by the App.

6. App Updates

Digital Card checks for a newer version two ways: (1) through Google Play's standard update mechanism, if you installed the app from Google Play — this works the same as any other Play Store app update; and (2) by looking for an update file already present on your device's storage, which involves no server contact at all — it only reads a file that already exists locally, if any. Installing an update always requires your explicit confirmation.

7. Home Screen Widget

If you add the Digital Card widget to your home screen, it displays one of your own cards (whichever you've starred, or your most recently edited one) so you can share it without opening the app. The widget reads this data from the same local, on-device database as the rest of the App — nothing about the widget sends your card data anywhere. Cards you've received from others are never shown on the widget.

8. App Lock

Digital Card offers an optional PIN and/or fingerprint/face lock. If you set a PIN, only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is stored on your device — the PIN itself is never saved anywhere, and we have no way to recover or view it. If you enable fingerprint/face unlock, that verification is handled entirely by Android's own operating system; Digital Card never receives, stores, or has access to your biometric data itself, only a success/failure result from the system.

9. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the card data you create with third parties, except as described in Section 4 (advertising) or when you personally choose to share a card through one of the App's sharing features.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your cards remain on your device until you delete them individually, delete the app's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling Digital Card removes all locally stored card data from your device. Backup files you've exported are not affected by uninstalling the App, since they live wherever you chose to save them.

11. Children's Privacy

Digital Card is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of Digital Card after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
