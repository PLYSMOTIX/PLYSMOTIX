Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Plysmotix Health ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the app. Plysmotix Health is developed by Plysmotix Tech Lab.

The short version: your health data — steps, water intake, sleep logs, heart rate readings, workout sessions, medicine reminders, and your weight/height/goals — is stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this data, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

1. Information the App Collects

Health and fitness data you enter or that the app measures, stored locally on your device:

Data typeWhat's stored
StepsDaily totals, and an hourly breakdown showing what time of day you were active
Workout sessionsStart/end time, type (walk/run/workout), and steps taken during the session
Water intakeAmounts you log, by day
SleepBedtime, wake time, and a self-rated quality score you enter
Heart rateReadings, if you connect a smartwatch or band via Health Connect (see Section 3)
Medicine remindersMedicine name, optional dosage note, and reminder time — see the note below
ProfileWeight, height, and your own goals (steps, water, heart rate alert range), used only to calculate distance, calories, and BMI on your device
A note on medicine data specifically: medicine names can indirectly reveal information about your health conditions. This data is treated the same way as your other health data described above — stored only on your device, never transmitted anywhere, never used for advertising, and never shared with any third party. It exists solely so the app can show you a local notification at the time you chose.

Information collected for advertising, via Google AdMob:

  • Advertising identifier and general device information, used to serve ads
  • Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a consent choice is collected via Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalized advertising data is used — see Section 4

Advertising data collection is completely separate from your health data. None of the health information described above is ever passed to AdMob or used to target ads.

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account registration information — the app does not require or support account creation.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To display your step count, water intake, sleep history, heart rate readings, workout sessions, and medicine schedule within the app
  • To calculate distance walked, calories burned, and BMI, based on the height/weight you provide
  • To send you local notifications you've opted into (step goal reminders, water reminders, bedtime reminders, medicine reminders, and heart rate range alerts)
  • To serve advertising through Google AdMob

3. Health Connect Data

If you choose to connect a smartwatch or fitness band, the app reads step count and heart rate data from Android's Health Connect — a system-level data store that your watch's own companion app (such as Wear OS, Samsung Health, Fitbit, or Garmin Connect) writes into.

Important: the app only reads from Health Connect. It never writes data back to Health Connect, and never shares this data with any third party. Heart rate data is used solely to display readings in the app and to check them against a range you set yourself — this is a simple numeric check, not a medical diagnosis, and the app is not a medical device.

You can revoke this app's access to Health Connect data at any time from the Health Connect app itself, under Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.

4. Advertising and Consent

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app will present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads, in line with applicable law. You can change your ad consent choice at any time from the app's Profile tab, under "Manage Ad Consent."

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's own Partner Sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

The app can send local notifications for: step goal progress, water reminders, bedtime reminders, medicine reminders, and heart rate range alerts. All of these are generated entirely on your device based on your own data and your own settings — none of them involve sending your data anywhere to generate the notification. You can turn off any of these individually from within the app.

6. Data Storage and Security

All health and fitness data is stored locally on your device using a local database and app preferences. It is not transmitted to, or stored on, any server we operate. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device (unless your device's own backup settings separately back up app data — you can control this in your device's system settings).

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

  • You can disable step tracking, water reminders, bedtime reminders, medicine reminders, or heart rate alerts at any time from within the app
  • You can revoke Health Connect access from the Health Connect app at any time
  • You can change or withdraw ad consent from the app's Profile tab at any time
  • You can delete individual medicines, workout sessions, or other logged entries directly within the app
  • You can delete all local app data by uninstalling the app, or by clearing app data from your device's system settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, as new features are added. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
